Context: the task form is specified as exactly three fields — title, body, tags (docs/specs/2026-09-13-tasks-tui-v1-design.md §6), and create mode deliberately invents no size, priority or complexity. `edit` takes `-p`, `--size`, `--complexity`, `--process` and `--no-complexity`/`--no-process` to clear; `--every`/`--no-every` and `--defer`/`--no-defer` are candidates to include or leave out.
